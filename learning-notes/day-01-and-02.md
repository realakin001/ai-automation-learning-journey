## Business Problem Practice

**Problem:** Restaurant staff manually manage customer reservations.

**Proposed workflow:**

1. Customer submits a booking form.
2. Check whether required information is present.
3. Save booking details in Google Sheets.
4. Notify the restaurant owner.
5. Owner reviews and confirms availability.

## Important Concepts

- Validate customer information.
- Check for duplicate bookings.
- Use human approval when necessary.
- Not every workflow needs AI.
